What it does

Built different.

Everything runs on your machine. No cloud. No logs. No one watching.

01

Stockfish Engine

Full Stockfish 16 compiled to WebAssembly. Analysis happens entirely on your device — no API calls, no servers, nothing external.

On-device · Zero latency
02

Auto-Move

Engine picks the best move and clicks it for you. Timing is randomized between 150–600ms so it never looks like a bot.

Human-like timing

Detects whether you're White or Black and only shows your moves. Never highlights what your opponent should play.

Your side only

Setup

Running in
two minutes.

No Chrome Web Store. No developer account. Just drop it in.

01

Download the zip

Hit the download button at the bottom of this page. Save QuickChess.zip anywhere — your desktop is fine. Don't unzip it, Chrome loads it directly.

02

Open Chrome Extensions

Open Google Chrome and type this in the address bar. Hit Enter.

chrome://extensions
03

Turn on Developer Mode

Find the toggle in the top-right corner of the extensions page that says Developer mode. Flip it ON. This is what lets Chrome load extensions from your computer.

Top-right corner of the page
04

Drag the zip in

Drag and drop QuickChess.zip directly onto the extensions page. Chrome installs it instantly. You'll see QuickChess appear in the list with a green toggle.

05

Start a game on chess.com

Go to chess.com, start any live game. The QuickChess panel appears automatically on the right side. It detects your color and shows your best move in green the moment it finishes analyzing.

Panel appears on the right side of the screen
06

Enable Auto-Move

Click ▶ Auto-Move in the panel to let QuickChess click the moves for you. For streaming on TikTok Live, press Shift+S before going live — the panel disappears entirely but the engine keeps running.
